CASE STUDY

RMS Rider

USTIAndroidiOS

About the project

RMS Rider is the rider-side app of a food delivery system, built for riders to go online, take orders, navigate to customers and settle cash.

Riders switch to Online to start receiving orders. From that moment the app shares their live location with the dispatch system, so orders go to the nearest available rider.

The app covers the full delivery flow: accepting an incoming order, confirming pickup from the restaurant, delivering to the customer and marking the delivery complete.

The route to the customer is drawn as a polyline on the map, and riders receive new orders and status updates through push notifications.

Riders can track and settle the cash they collect on delivery.

Location is sent to the server every 2 minutes, even when the screen is locked or the app is in the background.

Safeguards include a warning when GPS is switched off, automatic offline after a 15-minute grace period, and a maximum shift length.

Key achievements

  • Built reliable background location tracking using an Android foreground service and a position stream on iOS, managed by a single tracking manager.
  • Added a health check on every app launch and resume that restarts tracking if the operating system has stopped it.
  • Handled tracking failures with timeouts, no overlapping updates, a fallback to the last known position, and failure counting with rider warnings.
  • Kept the tracking layer independent of the UI, with Riverpod for state management and GoRouter for navigation.
  • Built a one-switch online and offline status that updates the server and starts or stops tracking.
  • Implemented the order flow from accepting an order to pickup confirmation and delivery completion.
  • Drew the live route to the customer as a map polyline.
  • Added push notifications for new orders and status updates through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Built cash settlement for tracking and settling the cash riders collect on delivery.
  • Added a guided permission flow with plain-language dialogs that explain why location access is needed.
  • Added multi-language support using slang.
